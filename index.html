The story behind the project

About Wano

Kano made computers feel welcoming, creative, and fun. Even though Kano's online services have gone away, the experience and the things people made with them are still worth keeping alive.

Wano is a project focused on bringing the Kano desktop, Story Mode, and activities back to modern Windows computers. The goal is simple: make it easy to jump in, explore, and create — without depending on the original online services.

Fully offline

Use the experience without relying on Kano's servers.

One file

A straightforward Windows package, designed to be easy to run.

Made for making

Keep the creative spirit of Kano alive on today's computers.
